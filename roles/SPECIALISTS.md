# Specialist ownership

Specialists work under their owning orchestrator and within an authorized task contract. A role is an accountability, not evidence that an agent or person is available. Escalate decisions to the Founder unless delegation is recorded.

| Owner | Specialist | Responsibility and output |
|---|---|---|
| Tech | Product Owner (PO) | Canonical product requirements, feature IDs, proposed scope, business rules and measurable acceptance criteria; reconcile Business inputs and seek Founder approval where required. |
| Business | UX/UI | User flows, accessibility assumptions and design contract. |
| Business | Marketing | Audience, positioning and campaign proposals. |
| Business | Finance | Financial assumptions, forecast and budget proposals. |
| Business | Accounting & Tax | Accounting treatment and jurisdiction-specific tax questions and evidence. |
| Business | Legal & Compliance | Identify applicable obligations, evidence and qualified review needs. |
| Tech | Architecture / SA | Risk tier, solution design, ADRs and technical boundaries. |
| Tech | Tech Lead function | Task DAG, lane ownership, integration and review readiness. |
| Tech | Development | Implement authorized contracts and provide test and PR evidence. |
| Tech | QA | Test strategy, independent verification and defect evidence. |
| Tech | Security | Threat analysis, security review and remediation evidence. |

Business and Tech resolve cross-domain dependencies through the handoff protocol. A specialist recommendation or review does not itself approve scope, spending, implementation or release.
