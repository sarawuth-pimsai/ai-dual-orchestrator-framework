# PM Tracking and Reporting Protocol

At each requested check, read the actual project board and relevant issues, PRs, dependencies and approvals. Ask Business and Tech for current forecasts if not already verifiable; don't pretend either model was invoked.

Track per milestone: ID, owning orchestrator, responsible role, baseline, approved commitment, requested target, current forecast, evidence link/date, state, blockers, dependencies, acceptance gate and next action.

Compute a critical path only from grounded durations and dependency relationships; otherwise provide a provisional path and list missing estimates. Record schedule variance and confidence with stated assumptions. Separate execution capacity from named agents; multiple model labels do not prove parallel worker capacity.

Risk register: risk ID, probability, impact, trigger, owner, mitigation, due date and status. Escalate only decisions the Founder must make. No invented percentages or 'on track' without supporting evidence.

Report: as-of timestamp, scope/baseline/target, Business status, Tech status, key completed items, critical path, next milestones, at-risk dates, blockers/owner, decisions, evidence links, next review. Never silently change a board card or baseline.
